# Privacy Policy

**ClipRaven** — Clipboard Manager for Mac & iPhone/iPad  
**LumiBear Studio** · Last updated: May 2026

---

## Summary

ClipRaven does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## What We Collect

**Nothing.**

Clipboard history, tags, rules, and all other content you create in ClipRaven are stored locally in an encrypted database inside the app sandbox on your device. We have no access to this data.

---

## iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable iCloud Sync, your clipboard history is synced between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data flows only to your private iCloud account — it is never visible to us or any third party. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in Settings.

---

## Crash Reporting (Optional)

If you choose to enable crash reporting in Settings, the app uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to send anonymous diagnostic data when the app crashes. This includes:

- Crash stack traces
- Device type and OS version
- App version

It does **not** include clipboard content, personal identifiers, your name, or any other personal information. You can turn crash reporting off at any time in Settings.

---

## No Advertising, No Analytics, No Tracking

ClipRaven contains no advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, tracking pixels, or any form of behavioral data collection.

---

## Data Storage & Retention

All your data is stored locally on your device or in your private iCloud account. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. iCloud data can be deleted through your device's iCloud settings.

---

## Children's Privacy

ClipRaven does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
